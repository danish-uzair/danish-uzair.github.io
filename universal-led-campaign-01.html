Project Overview

This project focused on creating a powerful social media presence for Universal LED. From eye-catching posts to engaging stories and promotional banners, every design was crafted to highlight LED signage solutions and drive customer engagement.

Client
Universal LED
Industry
LED Signage & Solutions
Role
Graphic Designer / Visual Creator
Duration
May 2023 - June 2023
Tools
Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro

Challenge

The challenge was to make a technical and highly competitive niche like LED signage visually appealing and easy to understand for a broad audience while keeping the brand professional and trustworthy.

Solution

I designed bold, high-contrast visuals with clear layouts, impactful typography, and strong calls-to-action. Each design was optimized for different platforms and target audiences.
